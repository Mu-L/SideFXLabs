= Labs Quad Sphere =

"""Generates a quad spherical mesh."""

This node creates a sphere composed entirely of quadrilateral polygons.
The sphere is constructed from multiple sides, each of which can be subdivided independently to control surface smoothness while maintaining a quad-only topology.


@parameters

    Radius:
        #id: radius
        Radius of the sphere measured from its center.

    Subdivisions:
        #id: subdivisions
        Number of subdivisions applied to each side of the quad sphere. Higher values increase polygon density and smoothness.
    
    Center:
        #id: t
        Offset of cylinder center from object origin.

    Rotate:
        #id: r
        Rotation about the center of the cylinder.

    Add UVs:
        #id: adduvs
        Generates UV coordinates for each side of the sphere.
