"""Splits primitives into separate outputs based on their primitive normals."""

The Labs Split Primitives by Normal SOP evaluates the primitive attribute N and isolates primitives whose normals fall within a specified angular range around a given axis and direction.

This is useful for hard-surface modeling, scattering workflows, selective processing, or isolating regions of geometry based on surface orientation.

@parameters
    Axis:
        #id: axis
        The axis used to evaluate the orientation of primitive normals.

    Direction:
        #id: direction
        The direction along the specified axis that primitive normals must face to be included in the selection.
    
    Spread Angle:
        #id: spreadangle
        Primitives whose normals fall within this angle range are selected.

    Invert Selection:
        #id: negate
        Inverts the selection produced by the normal test.
