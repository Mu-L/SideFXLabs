= Labs Box Clip =

#type:      node
#context:   sop
#internal:  labs::box_clip
#icon:      DOP/emptyobject

"""Clips geometry using a resizable box-shaped region."""

:video:
    #src:/movies/boxclip.mp4

The Labs Box Clip SOP clips geometry against an axis-aligned box.
It provides controls for positioning and scaling the clipping region, selectively enabling clipping on individual sides, and optionally capping the resulting open faces.

@parameters
    Size:
        #id: size
        Per-axis scale of the clipping box.

    Center:
        #id: center
        Center position of the clipping box.

    Uniform Scale:
        #id: scale
        Uniform scale multiplier applied to *Size*.

    Bounding Axis:
        #id: boundingaxis
        Enables or disables clipping on each of the six sides of the box.

    Fill Holes:
        #id: fillholes
        Caps faces created by the clipping operation.
